## Upstream source

ReGlyph PDF is maintained in the fork network of **Tansuo2021/OCRPDF-TO-PPT**:

- Repository: https://github.com/Tansuo2021/OCRPDF-TO-PPT
- Inherited areas: desktop canvas and page editing, OCR integration, image helpers, and PowerPoint export.
- ReGlyph PDF additions: focused workflow, portable projects, strict edit contracts, scoped repair/compositing, batch/cache handling, transactional replacement and operation records, platform launchers, documentation and regression tests.
